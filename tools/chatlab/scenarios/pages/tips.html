Acme 8 kW hybrid inverter: five tips for the winter

1. Keep the battery above 20% at night: lithium cells age faster when they sit empty in the cold.

2. Set the grid charge window to the cheap night tariff, 23:00 to 07:00.

3. Update the Wi-Fi logger's firmware before the heating season.

4. Clean the fan filter once a month; the inverter derates when it runs hot.

5. Use the "zero export to CT" mode if the grid operator does not pay for export.
